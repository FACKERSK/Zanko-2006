ZANKO 2006 - Afterglow  (v0.9.0, the west city, the station, Odawara streets)

- 0.9.0: the town grows west; a station; new clothes, weapons
  and creatures.
  * The high-rise district beyond the west tunnel: the coast
    road's tunnel now goes through the headland to towers of
    8 to 22 storeys, shop rows and bars on a waterfront
    boulevard. More creatures there, and more in every
    container. The world is 290 m wider; the bay runs on west.
  * Kirimachi station: the station house (concourse, ticket
    machines, gates, ticket office, kiosk), a 64 m platform
    with a canopy, benches and vending machines, and a
    two-car train stopped at it that you can walk through.
  * Basements under many flats, shops and offices: stairs
    down to storage rooms with better loot.
  * Streets: no more square 90-degree corners; every bend and
    every corner where two streets meet is rounded. Zebra
    crossings and stop lines at junctions, traffic signals on
    the avenues, centre and edge lines, white-lined narrow
    lanes without pavements, tall street lamps, more overhead
    wires, portal gates at the bridges (after Odawara).
  * Houses after Odawara street photos: low hipped tile roofs
    with deep eaves, pent roofs, door hoods, block walls with
    metal fences, garage shutters, carports. The 16 house
    presets are replaced.
  * Doors open and shut: [E] at any building door. A quarter
    of the shut ones are locked and have to be forced.
  * Japanese room layouts: genkan with shoe cabinet and step,
    LDK, tatami rooms with tokonoma, butsudan and oshiire,
    bathroom, toilet and washroom, western bedrooms upstairs.
  * 16 new weapons (wakizashi, naginata, bokken, golf club,
    shovel, machete, cleaver, kama, pipe wrench, police baton,
    tetsubo, pickaxe, scalpel, fire extinguisher, umbrella,
    stop sign) and 12 clothes in 4 new wear slots (head, body,
    legs, feet): protection, warmth, some cost stamina.
  * 8 new creatures: kuchisake, crawler, bloated (bursts),
    tsuchigumo, nurse, riot police (armoured), gaki (packs),
    burned. More detailed creature skin, cloth and gore.
  * Map: sharp, smooth edges (rendered at twice the size,
    terrain every 4 m, built once).
  * Distance detail (LOD): the town is drawn in 64 m chunks,
    far ones as simple blocks in the fog; higher limits for
    lights, signs and creatures.
  * Saves from 0.8.5 do not carry over (the town changed).


- 0.8.5: the Mod Maker grows up; busier buildings.
  * 89 building presets (houses, flats, shops, restaurants and
    bars, konbini, koban, police station, hospital, offices,
    hotels, station, warehouses) placed from a Buildings tab:
    move, turn, storeys, preset, wall colour.
  * Buildings carry balconies with laundry, AC units, pipes,
    water tanks, dishes, roof fences, outside stairs, awnings,
    shutter boxes, tiled gable roofs, bicycles, mailboxes. The
    town's own buildings get them too.
  * Terrain tab: raise, lower, flatten, smooth and erase
    brushes; saved with the mod.
  * Exact editing: a right panel with typed X / Y / Z, size and
    angles; blocks can tilt and lean (walkable); editable snap
    steps; arrow keys and PgUp/PgDn nudge.
  * Road ends snap to roads; street ends on street ends join
    into one street; split a street.
  * Model viewer, Items tab (weapon and item stats), undo and
    redo, F1 help, tab groups (World, Objects, Assets, All),
    save prompt on close.

- 0.8.4: building in the Mod Maker, the mountain tunnel.
  * Mod Maker Build tab: insert blocks and props (trees,
    cherry, garden pine, cedar, vending machine, car, lamp
    post), move them with arrows, turn them, size them with
    handles, snap to 0.5 m / 15 degrees, pick any texture and
    colour, solid or not, duplicate and delete. Parts are
    saved with the mod and built into the town with collision.
  * Map tab: dragging a point adds points around it so roads
    and lines bend as smooth curves; the town rebuilds when the
    point is let go.
  * The old tunnel at the end of the touge runs into a real
    mountain rising behind the portal.
  * The seawall, its railing and the sea-side pavement run
    unbroken along the whole coast road; the river water ends
    at the coast road.
  * No more ground slopes hanging into tunnel mouths; raised
    pavements stop short of junctions.

- 0.8.3: the Mod Maker, kerbs, the seawall.
  * Mod Maker (Root/ModMaker.exe, or Open Mod Maker in the
    launcher's Mods tab): a map editor that flies through the
    town in game graphics. Drag the points of the coast road,
    streets, railway, touge, river, sea, town outline, bridges,
    lots, parks and spots; add and delete points, make new
    streets, change street width and type, rebuild the town
    in place and save it as a mod. Textures and models are
    replaced by dropping a .png or .obj onto the window. The
    Mod tab saves name, author, version and description and
    exports a .zip to share.
  * The map is a file now (Data/map.zmap); a mod can carry its
    own. Multiplayer refuses a player with a different map.
  * Streets have raised pavements with a kerbstone and a kerb
    face; the coast road has raised pavements on both sides.
  * The seawall stands along the whole coast road again, with
    a sand beach at its foot sloping into the water (no more
    grass shelf hanging over the sea). The river ends at the
    coast road instead of running out into the bay.
  * Food, drinks and medicine are taken into the hand (right
    click in the bag or the quickbar key) and used with the
    attack button. Nothing is used up, and no animation plays,
    when you are full, not thirsty or there is nothing to treat.
  * The phone opens and closes when the flashlight goes on and
    off; a new knife inspect; fists held in a guard.
  * Stop lines only where both ends are on the street.

- 0.8.2: settings, the old tunnel, trains.
  * Settings has tabs: Video, Audio, Gameplay, Controls and
    Accessibility.
  * Video: VSync, frame rate limit (30 to 240 or unlimited),
    sharp or smooth upscaling, PS1 vertex wobble on/off,
    brightness, fog, FPS counter.
  * Audio: separate effects, ambience and interface volumes;
    mute when the window loses focus.
  * Gameplay: view bob, weapon sway, camera shake, hold or
    toggle sprint and sneak, crosshair style and colour, hit
    markers, tutorial hints, auto-reload, centred weapon,
    pause when the window loses focus, insanity events on/off.
  * Controls: invert mouse Y; key bindings from here.
  * Accessibility: reduce flashing, damage overlay strength,
    insanity colour filter strength.
  * The old tunnel at the top of the pass is cut into the
    mountain: the road runs straight in through a rock cutting
    to a brick portal; inside it is brick-lined with brick ribs.
  * The railway tunnels have track, sleepers and walkways
    instead of road, and a commuter train stopped inside where
    the power died. No more cars on the rails.
  * Building: the mouse wheel turns the piece (15 degree steps,
    hold Shift for 5); R picks the next piece. Inside or next to
    a turned building the piece lines up with its walls first.
- 0.8.1: sounds.
  * Revolver: the hammer cocks on the draw and after every shot;
    the reload is heard step by step (crane out, empties
    punched out and landing, speedloader in, spin, crane shut).
  * Inspects have sounds: blade ringing, knife flips, the bat
    on the shoulder, knuckles cracking.
  * Jumping and landing, eating, drinking, pills, bandaging,
    sleeping, the phone alarm when you wake rested, TVs and
    tapes, manga pages, the map, the bag, rummaging while you
    search, map markers.
  * Multiplayer: other players' shots, swings and footsteps
    are heard where they are.
  * Greyed-out buttons buzz when clicked.
  * Insanity: with a loaded revolver in hand, once a minute
    there is a 1% chance your hand turns it on you.
- 0.8.0: multiplayer.
  * Up to 8 players in one town. Main menu > Multiplayer: one
    player hosts, the others join with the host's IP (UDP port
    27015 by default; over the internet forward the port or use a
    VPN such as Radmin VPN).
  * Co-op: creatures hunt whoever is nearest; doors, windows,
    barricades, junk, containers and everything built are shared.
  * The host can let players hurt each other (PvP).
  * If you die you respawn; the town goes on.
  * Containers hold the same things for everyone in a session.
  * Inventory panels keep a clear gap: names in a container no
    longer vanish when the mouse is over it.
  * Quickbar numbers moved off the item names.
  * Every road walked end to end: no more falling through the
    coast road or its sidewalks, no invisible walls on streets
    (stray bridge railings, a seawall cutting the road at the
    tunnel mouths, cars and poles in the middle of the street).
  * The station no longer blocks the streets around it; level
    crossings where roads cross the railway.
  * The pass looks like a mountain road: wooded slopes, brush
    along the verges, and the town opens up below it.
  * Foliage across town: potted plants at front doors, garden
    trees, street trees on the avenues, cherry trees along the
    river, pines on the coast road, shrubs between houses.
  * No more stone walls across the coast road at its bends.
  * Coin parking lots, curve mirrors at junctions, stop lines,
    zebra crossings, overhead wires, post boxes, tsunami signs.
  * New revolver: six-chamber fluted cylinder with the rounds
    in it, crane, ejector, hammer and trigger that move. Drawn
    from the holster and thumb-cocked; the hammer falls and the
    cylinder turns with every shot. Reload: swing out, eject the
    empties, speedloader, spin, flick shut. New inspect.
  * New inspects for the katana, knife, bat / pipe / crowbar and
    axe / sledge.
  * Weapons lag and settle on springs when you turn, lean into
    strafes, dip when you jump and land.
  * Hit markers on the crosshair: white body, yellow head, red
    kill, with a tick.
  * Thumbs are joined to the hands.
  * Controls: every key can be rebound (Menu > Controls): two
    keys or mouse buttons per action plus a gamepad button.
    Saved in Data/keybinds.ini. On-screen hints show your keys.
  * Gamepad support: left stick move, right stick look, triggers
    attack and guard; in menus the stick moves the cursor, A
    clicks, B goes back, Start pauses.

- 0.6.0: Zanko 2006. The town as drawn.
  * New name: Zanko 2006. The story is now set in 2006.
  * Windows installer (no administrator rights needed). The
    launcher now updates the game by itself from GitHub: it
    downloads the new installer, runs it and reopens. Saves,
    accounts and settings are kept. Turn it off in Options.
  * Hands: the left hand holds a flip phone, its light is your
    flashlight; the right hand holds fists or the weapon. New
    revolver model; melee weapons rest lower and tilt forward;
    guarding raises the blade across the body.
  * The town is fixed: the same streets, buildings and rooms every
    run, built from the 5b map: a curved edge, a ring road, a
    diagonal avenue across the grid, the old town and fish market
    on the bay, and the coast road wrapping around them. It is
    about twice as large as before.
  * Every street end runs on to the ring road, the avenue or the
    coast road. Walkways line them with hedge planters, wooden
    bollards and trees.
  * Rooms are furnished to a plan instead of at random: kitchens
    (fridge, cabinets, sink, stove, dining table), tatami living
    rooms (low table, cushions, bookcase, chest), bedrooms
    (futons, wardrobe, desk), shops (shelving, counter, register,
    stockroom), konbini (drinks wall, aisles, magazine rack,
    register, ATM, copier), offices (desk islands, lockers,
    whiteboard, water cooler) and the koban.
  * HUD: every gadget is a small 3D glass slab that sways with
    your head.
  * Windows Vista style buttons and caption buttons.
  * Map colours follow the town map: green hills, blue bay,
    dark roads, pale buildings, brown old houses.
  * Old saves do not load: the world changed.
- 0.5.0: a new city layout.

  * Old town: narrow alleys, small houses, paper lanterns and

  * Coast road: two lanes along the bay with a seawall, beach,

    into a road tunnel at each end.
  * Three road tunnels (west, south-east, north). You can walk

  * Fixes: furniture no longer blocks doors; empty quickbar slots

- 0.4.0: the basics every survival game has.
  * Saving: automatic on sleep, every 2 minutes and on Main Menu.
    Continue loads it. Death deletes it.
  * Day and night: 20-minute days. Grey overcast days, dark
    nights. City spirits only walk at night; more mobs at night.
  * Traits: pick one strength and one weakness at New Game.
  * Crafting (Tab): bandages, splints, boiled water, charcoal,
    blessed ofuda, nail bat, weapon repair.
  * Water: rain barrels, a park fountain and hill springs. Fill
    empty bottles; boil unboiled water at a fire.
  * Sickness from bad water (vomiting, faster hunger/thirst).
  * Infection from open wounds and rags; fever blocks recovery.
    Disinfectant cures it.
  * Broken legs from falls and heavy hits. Splint, then 12 h.
  * Weapons wear out and break. Repair with scrap + nails.
  * Garden beds grow daikon (about 1.5 days). Seeds in loot.
  * Spike traps and can alarms (Build menu).
  * Searched places far away sometimes restock at midnight.
  * Map (M) with buildings, subway, water, base and markers.
  * First-time tips. Not yet: vehicles, multiplayer.
- 0.3.5: ambient music. Three looping tracks (city, hills,
  subway) crossfade by location and dip during chases. Music
  volume slider in Settings. Spirits now give up: 3 s without
  sight or 40 s of chasing, then they drift away, ignore you for

- 0.3.4: spirits and screamers no longer look alike. Spirits glow
  pale blue, float, and two blue soul-flames circle them. Swings
  only report "passes through" when no solid mob is in reach and
  nothing blocks the blow; you can no longer hit through walls.
- 0.3.3: no more wallhacks. Mobs only see and hit you with a
  clear line of sight; noise brings them to the sound, not to you.
- 0.3.2: mouse shows in the pause menu, quickbar is drag and drop
  (Tab), ground and floors rebuilt to stop flickering,
  switchback stairwells, room walls no longer cut through doors.
- 0.3.1: stairwells visible from upper floors, Esc menu opens,
  heavier hits (longer freeze, roll, flash, flinch, more blood).

- Inventory: hands, back, pockets and bags (school bag, duffel,
  hiking pack). Weight slows walking, swings and stamina.
- Loot is searched, not picked off the floor: hold E on
  cabinets, fridges, wardrobes, desks, lockers, car trunks.
- No health bar. Wounds bleed: bandage or rag them, then you
  heal slowly. Food, water and blood drain faster.
- Skills level with use: Strength, Fitness, Carpentry,
  Scavenging, Stealth, First Aid.
- Buildings have up to 4 floors, rooms and stairs, some
  choked with junk you must smash through.
- Subway under road 3: three stairwells, a dead train,
  lockers, and the things that live down there.
- Leave the city and the hills of the old wallpaper appear.
  They eat your mind. Spirits gather there.
- New mobs: stalker, screamer, mannequin, hanger, attendant.
  Hits freeze, shake and throw blood.
- Base building: walls, door walls, floors, chests, beds,
  campfire. Raise a Kamidana; spirit wards work only
  inside its 22 m.
- Spirits are rarer and only hunt what they can see.
