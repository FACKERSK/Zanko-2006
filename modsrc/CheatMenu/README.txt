CHEAT MENU for Zanko 2006 (game v0.7.1 or newer)

Install: drag CheatMenu.zip onto the launcher window, or copy it into the game's Mods folder.
Use: press F8 during play. The game pauses while the menu is open. F8 or Esc closes it.

Toggles
  God mode            no damage, no bleeding, no broken bones, no sickness or infection
  Infinite stamina
  No needs            food, water, warmth, rest and sanity stay full
  Infinite ammo       the revolver never needs reloading
  Unbreakable         the weapon in your hands never wears out
  One-hit kills       every hit kills (spirits still need an ofuda)
  Noclip              fly through walls and terrain: WASD where you look, Space up, Ctrl down, Shift fast
  Peaceful            no creatures
  Freeze the clock
  Endless battery     the flashlight never runs out
  Move speed          0.5x - 4x

Actions
  Heal fully, kill creatures within 60 m, set the time (morning, noon, dusk, midnight),
  teleport to your last map marker or the station,
  give a katana, a revolver with 24 rounds, medicine, food and water, a building kit,
  ofuda and salt, a hiking pack, batteries.

Settings: cheats.ini sets the key and which toggles start switched on.
Disable: untick "Cheat Menu" in the launcher's Mods tab. Without the mod the menu does not exist.
