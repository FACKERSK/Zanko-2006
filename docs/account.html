DownloadAccount

Account市民登録

One account for the launcher: a username, an e-mail and a password. The e-mail has to be confirmed by the link sent to it before the launcher lets the account in.

3 to 16 characters: letters, digits, _ and -. Shown in the game.

At least 8 characters.

Accounts are kept by Google Firebase Authentication: the e-mail, the username and a hash of the password. Nothing else is stored.
